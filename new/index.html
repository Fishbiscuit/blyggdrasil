Plant your tree.

Four steps, about five minutes. Your keys are made in this browser and never leave it: this page sends nothing anywhere except one read of your blyg’s public profile.

  1. Your blyg

    The trunk of your tree. Don’t have one? Start a blyg first.

  2. Your keys

    A main key signs your tree. A recovery key, kept offline, can take over if the main key is lost. Save both files somewhere safe: anyone with them can sign as you.
