      What you're seeing

      • A lit window is one of a blyg's ten most recent posts. It is brightest on the day it was published and dims over the following weeks.
      • A blue window is a post that includes AI-generated passages. The post names the model.
      • A flickering window is a post that was revised after it was published.
      • Shutters mark a withdrawn post. The publisher asked readers to stop showing it.
      • Lanterns by the door mark a burst: a thread and its fragments published as one act.
      • A string of lights joins two blygs that have quoted each other. Each bulb is one quote, in the order they were published.
      • A dark house is a blyg that doesn't let browsers read it. Quotes from its neighbours still reach its door.

      Neighbours are the blygs that quote each other. Nothing here counts followers, likes or posts, and houses aren't sized by activity.

      Your browser reads the public directory and then each blyg's own manifest, index and recent items, following the Blygger v0.4 layout. Nothing is copied to a server. Text appears as plain text with a link back to the exact post.
