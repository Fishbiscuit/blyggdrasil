One tree for everywhere you post.

Your blyg is the trunk. Your accounts on X, Bluesky, Mastodon and GitHub are branches, each one proven by a line you post there. A key you hold lets the whole tree move with you if a platform drops you.

    Signed posts carry a signature by this tree’s key over their exact text, so they stay attributable wherever they’re copied.

      Connect

      Make a key, list your accounts in one signed post on your blyg, and put one line with your key on each account. Anyone can check every link, in their own browser.

      Reinstate

      Lose an account, a domain or even the key? A new account proves itself with the same key, a new blyg keeps your proofs, and a recovery key you set aside takes over.

      Reconstitute

      Bring your public posts from X and Bluesky back to your own blyg with their original dates, linked to where they first appeared. Private messages and likes stay on your machine.

      Verdicts mean exactly what they say: a verified branch names this tree’s key or links back to the blyg. Blyggdrasil never counts, scores or ranks anyone, and adds nothing to Blygger’s protocol. It builds Blygger’s own identity practice.
