Blygger began as Ygg, named for Yggdrasil.
This gives the tree back its name.

Blygger keeps identity out of its protocol, and so does Blyggdrasil: it adds nothing to the wire. It builds the practice Blygger already wrote down, decision #35, “a person is a URL they control”, with both of its proofs: a link back, and a signature over each post’s exact text.

Verdicts mean exactly what they say. A lit branch names this tree’s key or links back to the blyg. Nothing is counted, scored or ranked. Read the spec.
